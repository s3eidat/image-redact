Image Redact

Cover names, emails, card numbers or faces in a screenshot, then save a brand-new image. The covered pixels are replaced in the exported file, not just hidden on top. Paste a screenshot (Ctrl+V) or drop an image; it never leaves your browser.

Heads-up: blur and pixelate over text can sometimes be reversed or guessed, especially for short numbers. For passwords, card numbers and names, use solid Black.
